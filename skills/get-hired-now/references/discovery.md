# Live discovery, qualification and research

## Discover actual postings

Read supplied targets, eligibility, constraints, existing applications and the
requested batch boundary. Use actual web/search/job-source tools. Search the chosen
sources and official employer career pages; follow promising listings to the
official requisition. Do not manufacture a jobs.json requirement for the user.

For each unique posting, save employer, title, requisition ID, canonical URL,
source URL, actual hiring geography, arrangement, observation time, posting-date
evidence and material requirement quotes. Distinguish initial publication from
repost/update dates. “Remote” and an employer's country do not prove eligibility.
For live application work, confirm open status and identity before investing in
tailored materials. If the person explicitly requests local tailoring against a
supplied posting and live tools are unavailable, complete that supported work and
record current availability/identity verification as unknown. It is not submission-ready.

Preserve a resumable queue. De-duplicate against sightings, prepared applications,
attempts, confirmations and user-supplied exclusions/representation. Different
roles at one employer remain separate. Mirrors/city clones of one requisition do
not become extra opportunities. Do not promise an arbitrary application quota.

## Evidence-based fit

Map each material requirement to a concrete candidate fact and source. Use
`supported`, `partial`, `unknown` or `contradicted`. Separate required, preferred
and unclear wording. Read detailed supplied work/project evidence before crediting
a named tool, duration, degree, metric, certification or responsibility.

Disposition:

- `qualified`: required eligibility/evidence is supported. This is not permission
  to submit and does not establish compensation or form readiness.
- `conditional`: a required fact or genuine candidate decision is unresolved.
- `reject`: an evidenced mandatory mismatch or explicit candidate exclusion.
- `duplicate`: exact-role evidence establishes a previous record.
- `blocked`: an actual access, security, identity or tool limitation.

Missing evidence does not justify “the candidate has never done this.” Preferred
gaps do not become mandatory failures. Do not add overlapping roles into extra
years. Preserve contradictory geography and ask about material conflicts.

## Economics and research

Check the official posting for pay/arrangement and, when useful and accessible,
compare a credible salary source. Separate employer budget, market estimate and
candidate expectation. Keep market, currency, period, gross/net/base/total,
employment arrangement, date and confidence visible. Do not substitute a different
hiring market or silently convert contractor expectations into employee salary.

An unknown budget is not a rejection. Record economics as `unknown` and use the
person's undisclosed-pay policy. An observed budget below a hard floor is a material
conflict; never lower their floor without a supplied correction.

## Production investment

For scan/evaluate-only requests, return the screened queue or grounded evaluation
without producing CVs for every result. For preparation/application requests:

- `none`: rejected/unresolved eligibility, known required-fact/security blocker.
- `reuse_only`: qualified with a feasible visible form, but pay/arrangement unknown;
  adapt supported reusable material proportionately.
- `tailored`: demonstrated fit, feasible form and supported/plausible economics.

An explicit request for deeper tailoring can increase local investment. It cannot
change facts, permissions or a hard gate. Research only what will change a decision;
continue other qualified jobs when one role blocks.
