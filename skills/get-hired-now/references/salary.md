# Salary research and expectation decisions

Use the candidate's current arrangement-specific policy and exact employer history.
Keep employer budget, comparable estimate, desired expectation and amount actually
entered separate. A prior exact-role exception is not a universal lower floor.

For a substantial round, assign one read-only salary owner per exact role while
form mapping and useful preparation continue. Main owns the final expectation.
Small tasks can use the same AI sequentially. Unknown budget or legal arrangement
limits ordinary effort to `reuse_only`; it is not automatic rejection.

## Research handoff

Check official disclosed pay first and try a salary aggregator or another credible
comparison. Record inaccessible/absent data honestly; a blocked aggregator does
not stall every role or justify invented numbers. Return:

- Employer, exact title/requisition, seniority, hiring market and arrangement.
- URLs, retrieval/source dates and sample sizes/limitations when available.
- Currency, pay period, gross/net, base/total, expected hours and benefits.
- Employer-disclosed band separately from third-party comparable bands.
- Confidence, comparability gaps, recommended expectation and rationale.

Employer-disclosed pay takes precedence over an aggregator parse. Do not substitute
a headquarters-market salary for the actual hiring market. Contractor and employee
packages need separate comparison; paid leave, benefits, hours and taxes affect the
comparison. Never mechanically convert a contractor monthly target into employee
base pay or present contingent task/bonus pay as guaranteed salary.

Before entering a required number, reconcile the research with the authorized
range/floor, the form's basis and an amount already communicated for this role.
Avoid changing an employer expectation silently. Choose an evidence-backed value
within policy rather than automatically selecting either endpoint. If data is
sparse, a value permitted by the person's policy can be used as their expectation,
with uncertainty disclosed; it is not a discovered market band. If no authorized
policy supports an answer or the package falls outside it, ask only for that role's
decision and continue others. Record the chosen value/basis and reasoning with the
actual form evidence. Optional unknown salary fields can remain blank.
