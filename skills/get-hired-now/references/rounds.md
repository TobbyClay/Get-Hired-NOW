# Productive rounds and resumable carryover

Use this reference for a full search/application round, pipeline, or requested
strategy improvement. A scan ends with a screened queue. Prepare and apply advance
only the requested stages. Updating strategy does not launch a round or scheduler.

## Configure the search from this candidate

Build a role-family map from supplied work and targets. Examples include project/
program delivery, product ownership, technical leadership, implementation,
automation/business systems, reporting/data operations, technical customer support
and software/integrations. These are options, not eight mandatory lanes for everyone.
Use title synonyms and problem/responsibility queries. Include general titles and
adjacent levels when concrete evidence transfers; verify the actual responsibility
behind a Manager, Head or Director title. Do not invent budgets, reports or tenure.

Record primary and exploratory families, initial effort, geography, arrangements,
source routes and original-publication policy. Search recent supply first. Only
use a maximum age, numeric targets, time budget or lane weighting supplied by this
candidate or agreed for this round. Never copy another workspace's values. An
unspecified age limit remains unset rather than silently rejecting older open roles.

Use a mix of available routes: job connectors/authenticated boards, direct employer
or ATS boards, startup/remote/regional boards, and targeted professional-network/
public web searches. Do not require a particular vendor or a new account. Check
filter support and pagination; a request parameter is not proof it was applied.
Keep source cursors and prior checks to reduce unchanged duplicates. Recheck roles
that advance. Rotate low-yield queries and replenish fresh employers.

An application goal is not a hard cap unless the person says so. Reaching it alone
does not end an otherwise authorized round. Never invent acceptances or weaken
eligibility, pay, age, security or fact constraints to reach it. Honor explicit time
limits and reserve enough time to save outcomes. If no limit was supplied, use a
bounded batch and available runtime; carry unfinished work forward rather than
claiming an unlimited search or an exhausted market.

## Keep the pipeline moving

1. Read current facts, policy, receipts and the carryover queue. Check any active
   round/reservations before starting overlapping work. Finish or assign disjoint
   roles; do not replay a completed round.
2. Reserve exact identities before expensive work. Scouts return literal public
   evidence. Fit review separately maps requirements to candidate sources. Merge
   mirrors and city variants by internal requisition when established.
3. Check current official status, geography, original age under the selected policy,
   known pay conflicts and representation. Inspect the public form before extensive
   writing: mandatory profile links, degrees, travel, tests, video, unfamiliar
   declarations, authentication and accepted uploads can determine feasibility.
   Hidden branches stay unknown until the submission owner inspects them.
4. Assign preparation level and a named verified resume source. Known required-fact,
   eligibility or security blockers usually receive `none`; unknown pay/arrangement
   with otherwise plausible fit receives `reuse_only`; supported fit, feasible form
   and plausible market-correct economics can justify `tailored`. An explicit local
   tailoring request can override effort, never truth or readiness.
5. Research salary and map forms independently while useful permitted preparation
   continues. Stream leads and completed packets in small groups, for example two
   to four. Submit each suitable ready role under current authorization; do not wait
   for every worker or a complete shortlist.
6. Bound supported technical recovery, usually one or two useful checks. A genuine
   blocker saves that role with a specific next action while others advance. An
   asynchronous final submit gets time and inspection; another Submit is never a
   diagnostic. Deferred verification/security holds remain deferred until resumed.
7. Before ending below goal, cover the selected primary families and available
   routes or name the time/capability/interruption that prevented it. Save final
   actual outcomes and carryover. Do not stop at an arbitrary small provisional
   count while feasible authorized work remains within the batch/time boundary.

## Queue contract

Each carryover item names exact identity/official URL, family/source, last check,
requirement/fact evidence, age decision, economics, preparation level, form
feasibility, existing artifact, blocker, next action, owner and recheck trigger.

Distinguish an actionable lead from a conditional hold and a submission-ready
packet. Twenty saved questions are not twenty ready applications. Store closed,
excluded and duplicate roles with their reason so the next run avoids rediscovery.
A hold advances only when its specific new fact, access or employer evidence arrives.
Uncertain attempts belong to receipt reconciliation, not the ordinary apply queue.

## Files and measurements

Use the candidate's existing layout when available. Otherwise create a private
dated batch with `sources/`, `research/`, `packets/`, `packets/previews/`, `attempts/`,
`receipts/`, `work/` and a results file. Keep final files separate from disposable
renders. Continue an existing exact role in its batch; never overwrite a submitted
version or lose the original evidence location.

Report these separate quantities, with source and family coverage:

| Quantity | Evidence that earns it |
| --- | --- |
| Raw discoveries | Actual sightings, possibly repeated or partially collected |
| Unique official screens | Exact posting read, material requirements checked and duplicates searched |
| Qualified / conditional | Requirement-to-candidate mapping and unresolved conditions recorded |
| Prepared | Actual final files with claims and required QA reviewed |
| Ready | Complete live form and current eligibility/answers/pay/artifact checks passed |
| Attempted | Distinct roles with a final Submit action; autosave/upload alone is not final Submit |
| Unresolved / failed / security-blocked | Observed result, separately reason-coded |
| Confirmed | Exact-role employer acceptance evidence, deduplicated |
| Recruiter progress | A separately observed reply, interview, rejection or other recruiting event |

Counts may overlap across stages and are never summed into applications. Report
unique exact roles as well as raw actions. A late receipt can confirm a prior
attempt; identify that cohort instead of calling it a new application today.

Record wall elapsed time separately from measured active operator time. Parallel
worker minutes are not the same denominator. If only wall time exists, call the
rate confirmations per elapsed hour; leave active-hour rates unavailable. Do not
invent time lost per blocker. Track reason-coded attrition: duplicate, closed,
geography, age, pay, mandatory qualification, missing fact, account route, security,
assessment and technical failure. End with an explicit stop reason and next actions.

After several comparable rounds, inspect source/family yields and recruiting
cohorts. Rotate effort toward useful fresh supply while preserving exploratory
coverage. Receipt volume alone does not establish improved hiring outcomes.
